QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are there any hidden sign-up or enrollment fees?

No, we operate with a transparent pricing model. There are no surprise enrollment fees or sign-up charges added to your membership.

Can I temporarily freeze my membership?

Yes, members can pause their membership for up to 3 months per year in case of travel, medical conditions, or other personal commitments. Freeze requests require 7 days written notice.

Do you offer personal training for beginners?

Absolutely! All new members receive a complimentary onboarding and assessment session where a personal coach guides you through layout orientation, proper equipment forms, and custom advice.
