THE PULSE FIT STORY

WE ARE NOT JUST A GYM. WE ARE A SANCTUARY OF STRENGTH.

Founded in 2014 in the heart of Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, PULSE FIT was established with a singular mission: to eliminate mediocre fitness standards and build an elite environment where ambition meets cutting-edge methodology.

Spanning over 25,000 sq. ft. of space, our facility features competition-grade iron, specialized athletic turfs, and bio-metric recovery suites tailored for beginners to professional athletes.

Our Mission

To empower individuals to transcend physical limits through expert guidance and world-class equipment.

Our Vision

To remain the premier fitness sanctuary known for innovation, community, and transformative results.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are there any hidden sign-up or initiation fees?

No! At PULSE FIT, we believe in complete transparency. What you see is what you pay. There are zero hidden contract initiation fees.

Can I pause or cancel my membership at any time?

Yes! You can pause your membership for up to 3 months for travel or medical reasons with 7 days written notice. Cancellations require no long-term lock-ins on monthly plans.

Are personal training sessions included in membership?

Our Pro Performance plan includes 2 sessions monthly, while our VIP Elite plan provides unlimited 1-on-1 coaching. Basic members can also purchase session bundles at discounted rates.
